01 // Executive Summary

The Month The Network Went Live

Season 2 was the program's second operating month — the season in which the Campus MVP network moved from a list of selected names to an active on-ground presence across campuses nationwide.

Each of these came in with supporting proof — player UIDs and college IDs, three-screenshot match sets, live Instagram links, and photo/video documentation. Season 2 produced both the activity and the record of it.
